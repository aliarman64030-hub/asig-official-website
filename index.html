Legal Governance & Client Service Terms

Binding Non-Disclosure (NDA)

All engagement details, manuscript drafts, author IPs, and audit evidence generated by ASIG remain strictly confidential under signed NDAs.

Retainer & Payment Terms

To initiate any publishing project or risk audit, clients must clear an advance retainer fee. Milestone payments are non-refundable once work has commenced.

Lawful Purpose & Non-Misuse

ASIG strictly conducts lawful audits and publishing setup. Clients guarantee that manuscripts submitted do not violate copyright laws or contain hate speech.

Contractor Identification

Field auditors and publishing associates operate under strict proxy protocols. Clients agree not to directly contact or bribe field associates assigned to their case.

Pre-Defined Project Scope

All audit, research, and publishing deliverables are strictly bounded by the agreed Statement of Work (SOW). Scope expansion or additional parameters require separate written approvals and supplementary fees.

Best-Effort Delivery & Partial Reporting

Intelligence and field verification operations are conducted on a legal, best-effort basis using open-source (OSINT) and accessible field data. If certain information is legally inaccessible or restricted, ASIG delivers verified partial findings with full transparency, which constitutes valid task completion.
