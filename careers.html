Structured Task-Based Remote Associate Program

Ali Sovereign Insights Group offers a transparent, stage-wise percentage distribution model for client acquisition, core execution, and quality control specialists.

Project Stage Commission & Revenue Share Distribution

  • Stage 1 — Client Onboarding & Discussion Specialist (15% Share):
    Assigned to the associate who acquires a new client OR conducts initial consultation and closes project terms with direct client inquiries.
  • Stage 2 — Primary Project Execution Lead (20% Share):
    Assigned to the core specialist managing primary task execution (editing, formatting, auditing, or core document preparation).
  • Stage 3 — Verification, Quality Report & Final Delivery (20% Share):
    Assigned to the compliance associate conducting final quality checks, preparing execution reports, and delivering completed assets to the client.
  • Settlement Schedule: Stage earnings are processed directly via banking channels (UPI/NEFT) within 5 to 7 business days following client milestone sign-off.
🔒 Data Security Assurance: ASIG strictly adheres to data protection guidelines. Applicants should NOT upload or submit public links containing government identity documents. Private identity verification is conducted securely only after preliminary profile selection.
