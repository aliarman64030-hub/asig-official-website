Legal Framework & Privacy Policy

1. Business Identity & MSME Registration

Ali Sovereign Insights Group (ASIG) is a legally registered Micro Enterprise under the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME), Government of India, holding Udyam Registration Number: UDYAM-I-UP-47-6427138.

2. Candidate & Client Data Protection

ASIG strictly respects applicant data privacy. We do not require, store, or demand the submission of unencrypted government identity documentation via public drive links during preliminary screening. Personal data provided during inquiries is maintained in confidential corporate records and is never sold or shared with third parties.

3. Operational Integrity & Authorization

All security auditing, simulation assessments, and corporate evaluations offered by ASIG are executed strictly under written contracts, defined scopes of work, and signed Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). Unauthorized security evaluations are strictly against enterprise policy.

4. Associate & Payment Terms

Disbursements to remote associates, freelancers, and project managers are executed directly upon task verification according to agreed project terms within 7 working days via official banking channels.
